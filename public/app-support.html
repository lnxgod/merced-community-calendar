A little help, close to home.

Help with the app, corrections to the calendar and public events we should know about.

Email will@gamechangersai.org. Game Changers AI provides Around Town as a free community service.

Suggest a community event

Post the organizer's public event link or flyer on our GameChangers AI Facebook page, or comment on one of our calendar posts. You can also email us. Events that meet these guidelines can be added to the app after we verify the details.

  • In Merced, Atwater or the McSwain neighborhood, our current Central Valley coverage.
  • Free or low-cost, welcoming and appropriate for families and the wider community. Include the price and any age or registration restrictions.
  • Public details we can verify: organizer, date, time, venue and a public link or flyer.
  • No casino or gambling outings, bar crawls, bar hopping or drinking-focused events in the app.
  • Mixed community events may include optional alcohol when families are welcome and alcohol is not the main activity. Published alcohol age limits still apply.

Suggestions are reviewed, not added automatically. Please leave out private messages, attendee names and personal information. To correct a listing, send its title and the organizer's updated public information.

Questions about an event?

Use the organizer link in the listing for tickets, accessibility, age restrictions, cancellations and other event questions. Game Changers AI helps people discover events; we do not host every event or handle its registration.

How listings stay current

The app downloads public listings from our community website when you open it or refresh and applies the app's event guidelines. The website and calendar subscription may include additional listings. Routine event updates do not require an app-store update. We check sources daily, but coverage is a selection and details can change. Check with the organizer before going.

When you are offline, the app shows a saved copy and a status message. Connect to the internet and refresh to get current listings. Saved events are kept on your device and are updated when a matching listing is downloaded.

Report an app problem

Tell us your phone model, operating-system version, app version and what happened. Include the event title if relevant. Screenshots can help, but remove private information first. Do not send passwords or identity documents.

Privacy and local data

No sign-in is required. You can unsave events or remove the app and its data using device settings. For details about public downloads, optional email requests and deletion, read our privacy policy.
