Around Town mobile app

A little help, close to home.

Help with the app, corrections to the calendar and public events we should know about.

Email will@gamechangersai.org. Game Changers AI provides Around Town as a free community service.

Suggest an event or a correction

Send a public organizer link or flyer, date, time, local venue and admission or registration details. We focus on Merced, Atwater and the McSwain neighborhood, especially free and low-cost things to do. Suggestions are verified before publication. Please leave out private messages, attendee names and personal information.

Questions about an event?

Use the organizer link in the listing for tickets, accessibility, age restrictions, cancellations and other event questions. Game Changers AI helps people discover events; we do not host every event or handle its registration.

How listings stay current

The app downloads the same public listings as our community website when you open it or refresh. Routine event updates do not require an app-store update. We check sources daily, but coverage is a selection and details can change. Check with the organizer before going.

When you are offline, the app shows a saved copy and a status message. Connect to the internet and refresh to get current listings. Saved events are kept on your device and are updated when a matching listing is downloaded.

Report an app problem

Tell us your phone model, operating-system version, app version and what happened. Include the event title if relevant. Screenshots can help, but remove private information first. Do not send passwords or identity documents.

Privacy and local data

No sign-in is required. You can unsave events or remove the app and its data using device settings. For details about public downloads, optional email requests and deletion, read our privacy policy.
