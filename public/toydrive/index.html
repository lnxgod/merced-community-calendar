From one bike to a family tradition

You may remember us as Team Charity Case. Today we run our family’s toy drive through GameChangers AI, the 501(c)(3) nonprofit we started to teach kids and adults how to use AI. The Christmas lights at our house have become part of that story, too.

  1. 1986

    Christmas at Will’s house

    Money was tight that year. People had donated gifts through the local firehouse, and when Will came downstairs on Christmas morning, a blue bicycle was waiting for him.

    Read Will’s original story

  2. At age 13

    Will starts collecting toys

    Will started his own collection through a firehouse in Columbus, Ohio. Friends and family pitched in. He kept the drive going after he moved to California, where car clubs and local businesses joined in. The Atwater fire department introduced him to the drive’s first local recipient, Merced County Operation Christmas Star.

  3. 2015–2017

    Filling the collection box

    We remember collecting toys in 2015. By December 2017, the gifts were piling up around the collection box in this photo from our family album.

  4. 2020

    Shopping carts full of gifts

    We collected $7,716.85 in cash donations and spent $7,728.08 on toys. That year we chose the toy drive to receive Team Charity Case’s competition prize.

  5. 2021

    Our first Christmas light show

    The lights joined the toy drive that Christmas. People gave $1,982 to our drive and $1,290 directly to Toys for Tots. We spent $1,992.46 on toys and took them to Merced County Fire.

    Watch the 2021 toy drive kickoff

  6. 2022

    The kids pitch in

    Our kids traded in some of their own games to help buy a gift. Store managers helped us stretch our shopping money, and we delivered more than $2,000 worth of toys to the firehouse. Supporters also gave $2,110 directly to Toys for Tots online.

  7. 2023

    A car full of Christmas gifts

    We spent $2,567.62 on toys and filled the back of our car with gifts. People gave $895 to our drive and another $960 directly to Toys for Tots.

    See the 2023 drive photo

  8. 2024

    A December of pickups and raffles

    The season started with a $25 donation. Our first pickup brought in more than $800 worth of toys. Local sponsors helped with raffles, and our December receipts show we bought at least $4,213.66 worth of toys.

    Read about the first pickup

  9. 2025

    Another blue bicycle

    Our receipts show we spent at least $6,778.88 on toys, and there was a blue bike among the gifts again. Our thank-you video used both Team Charity Case and GameChangers AI as we began teaching AI under our new name.

    Watch our 2025 thank-you

    A new show for the toy drive

    We used ChatGPT to bring favorite songs back to the yard and make new animations for the lights.
