From one bike to a family tradition

You may remember us as Team Charity Case. The Hatzer family drive continues under GameChangers AI, the 501(c)(3) nonprofit we started to teach kids and adults how to use AI to learn, solve problems, and create.

  1. 1986

    The blue bicycle

    Will was five when donations collected by his local firehouse helped his family through a tight Christmas. He came downstairs on Christmas morning to his first bicycle. It was blue.

    Read Will’s original story

  2. At age 13

    Giving back through a firehouse

    Will started collecting toys through a firehouse in Columbus, Ohio. Friends and family pitched in. Over the years, car clubs and local businesses joined, and Will carried the drive with him to California.

  3. By 2015

    The drive grows with the community

    Our family remembers collecting toys as far back as 2015. After Will moved to Atwater, the fire department introduced him to Merced County Operation Christmas Star, the drive’s first local recipient.

  4. 2017

    Filling the collection box

    Our family album shows a collection box full of donated toys in December 2017.

  5. 2020

    Shopping carts full of gifts

    We filled shopping carts with toys for the December drive.

  6. 2021

    Another firehouse drop-off, and a new idea

    We brought toys to Merced County Fire and tried our first Christmas light show, hoping it could help the drive in future years.

    Watch the 2021 toy drive kickoff

  7. 2023

    A car full of Christmas gifts

    Our 2023 photo shows how much a collection of individual gifts can add up to. We shared it with the story of the bicycle that started the drive.

    See the 2023 archive photo

  8. 2024

    A $25 start, then a packed first pickup

    The first donation was $25. By December, our first pickup alone brought in more than $800 worth of toys. Local sponsors helped keep the drive going with raffles.

    Read the first pickup update

  9. 2025

    Team Charity Case becomes GameChangers AI

    At the end of 2024, we shared plans for an education nonprofit. We started GameChangers AI to help people get hands-on with AI, especially in underserved rural communities. Our 2025 toy drive thank-you video carried both names. And that December, another blue bicycle was among the gifts.

    Watch our 2025 thank-you

  10. 2026

    We build a new show with AI

    This year we put what we teach into practice. We used ChatGPT to build our family’s Christmas light show, from mapping the lights to animating songs.

    See how we made the show
